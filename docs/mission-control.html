FirstHour

A box on the bathroom wall hears a fall, asks "are you okay?", texts the family a triage card and opens the door for help. No camera, and it keeps protecting with the Wi-Fi gone.

Why the name Half of older people who lay on the floor for an hour or more died within six months. The "long lie": Wild, Nayak & Isaacs, BMJ 1981. FirstHour's job is to get help moving in seconds, not an hour.

Concept dashboard with simulated data, built on the real contracts in our repo. Timings are illustrative except StepFun's 5–7 s, which we measured on our key. Margaret is fictional.

        How this wins

        Same product. Make the invisible parts visible.

        Our engineering is already stronger than our demo: signed commands, an offline-safe Pi, "silence is never fine", a Sentry scrubber that caught our own unlock code leaking. Judges only score what they can see in two minutes. Every item below either shows something that already works, or adds one utility aimed at a specific prize.

        Main track

        Beyond the Digital

        "Intelligence in the physical world… adaptive, interactive, immersive."

        Show
        • The scope and floor radar above: camera-free sensing you can watch.
        • Cut the Wi-Fi live; the box keeps alarming and counting, then replays its log with the real time down.
        • An LED ring on the box in the same state colors as the dashboard and phone.
        QNX

        Best use of QNX

        Hard rules: QNX OS, plus one AI module from oss.qnx.com.

        Show
        • Run the oss.qnx.com module on the Pi as an advisory third vote, drawn as a confidence track on the scope.
        • The two-sensor rule still decides, so we never trade safety for the prize.

        Gate 1 is 7:00 PM. If QNX isn't reading serial by then, drop it and say so.

        StepFun

        Get It Done with Step 5

        Must call step-5-preview at runtime. Judged on task completion, reliability, and whether users understand and control the workflow.

        Problem today
        • We measured 5–7 s per call against a 3 s deadline, so every call falls back and judges never see the model do anything.
        Fix
        • The alert stays on rules. The refinement is off the critical path, so give it a 12 s deadline and coalesce calls: one per answer burst.
        • Show rules vs model side by side (the panel above).
        • Caregiver Q&A agent with read-only tools over the live incident.
        Photon

        Messaging / calling agent

        "Agents that join human conversations… voice agents that pick up the phone… carry context to a messaging thread."

        Show
        • Sarah texts "is she still talking?" and gets an answer built only from the incident state.
        • Escalation ladder: second caregiver after 90 s, then a phone call that reads the triage card.
        • Judge enrollment: the judge texts JOIN to the line and becomes family. That also satisfies the shared line's "they text first" rule.

        Shared iMessage lines can't place calls. The call rung needs a Photon voice number; ask the table.

        Demo, two minutes

        The run of show

        1. 0:00Hook: the long lie. "Half of people who lie on the floor an hour or more are dead within six months. We get help moving in seconds, without a camera."
        2. 0:15The judge texts JOIN to the agent line. The operator taps Approve. The judge is now Margaret's family.
        3. 0:25Drop the beanbag. Both traces spike inside the fusion window, the box beeps and its clock starts.
        4. 0:35The voice asks "Are you okay?" A teammate answers "I slipped, I can't move my leg." The judge's phone buzzes with the triage card a few seconds into the fall.
        5. 0:55The judge texts "is she still talking?" and gets an answer from the live incident.
        6. 1:10A stranger's number texts UNLOCK 0000: refused on screen. The judge texts the real code; the box confirms and the door opens.
        7. 1:25Pull the Wi-Fi. The box keeps alarming and counting. Reconnect: the log replays with the real time down.
        8. 1:40Close on the Sentry trace with measured timings and the handoff card's QR.
        What does not change

        Guardrails

        Silence is never "fine"Only a clear "I'm fine" cancels.
        Two sensors decideThe QNX module and the radar only add confidence.
        The AI never decidesUnlocks, medications and the question script never come from a model.
        The Pi decidesSafety logic stays on the box. The cloud can only ask.
        No camera, everThe webcam is a microphone.
        Only measured numbersAnything we quote on stage came from a real run.
